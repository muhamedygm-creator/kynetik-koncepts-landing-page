What We Make

One philosophy, five ways to begin.

01

Bespoke One-of-One

A single car for a single client.

You bring the car you love, and the vision you hold for it. We bring that vision into being, refined in three dimensions until every detail is precisely yours. Nothing like it will exist anywhere else in the world.

02

Karbon Ikonics

Iconic silhouettes carried on modern platforms.

We take a proven platform, a Toyota Supra, a BMW Z4, and reclothe it in the silhouette of an icon, recreated in carbon fibre. Heritage above, factory reliability beneath. Produced in limited numbers.

03

Karbon Klassics

A classic reborn in carbon.

The first is the Karbon Kobra. The lines that made the original immortal, recreated in carbon and set upon modern underpinnings. A classic to be driven as one drives a modern car.

04

Karbon Elektric

Carbon transformation for the car you already drive.

For Porsche Taycan and Audi e-tron owners, we transform the car you already drive in carbon fibre, without disturbing what makes it dependable. The drivetrain remains factory. The character becomes wholly your own.

05

Karbon Toys

The same craft, made for the sand.

The Can-Am reimagined as a Lamborghini-styled machine, built for the dunes. Off-road craft that looks extraordinary and performs without fault.
