THE PROCESS

Design & Ideation

Every build begins as a sketch, refined until the shape feels right.

  • 01 Design & Ideation Every build begins as a sketch, refined until the shape feels right.
  • 02 3D Scanning & Modeling The vehicle is captured to sub-millimetre accuracy before a single cut is made.
  • 03 3D Printing Rapid prototypes prove the fit and form before carbon is ever committed.
  • 04 Prototyping A working test piece proves the geometry before it becomes permanent.
  • 05 Pre-Molding (Master Model) A master model is hand-finished to the exact surface the mold will carry.
  • 06 Mold Production Precision molds are produced from the master, built to hold that shape indefinitely.
  • 07 Carbon Production Carbon sheets are laid up and cured to the same standard on every build.
  • 08 Fitment Installation Every panel is test-fitted and installed by the same hands that built it.
  • 09 Paint & Finish A final protective finish, invisible in itself, permanent in what it protects.
